# Travel and Expense Policy

**Organization:** Northstar Technologies Pvt. Ltd.  
**Document Owner:** Finance  
**Version:** 4.1  
**Classification:** Internal  
**Effective Date:** 1 April 2026

> Fictional enterprise document created for semantic-search and RAG learning.

## 1. Purpose

This policy defines the rules for business travel and reimbursement of business-related expenses.

Employees are expected to use company funds responsibly and select reasonable travel options.

## 2. Travel Approval

Domestic business travel requires manager approval before booking.

International travel requires approval from both the employee manager and the relevant business-unit director.

Employees should use the company approved travel-booking platform whenever available.

## 3. Air Travel

Employees should book economy-class airfare for domestic and international travel.

Premium economy or business-class travel requires specific approval according to the travel-approval matrix.

Employees should normally select reasonable fares rather than flights based solely on preferred airlines.

## 4. Hotel Accommodation

Employees should book hotels within approved company limits.

If no suitable hotel is available within the approved rate, employees should obtain manager approval before booking a higher-cost property.

Personal hotel expenses such as minibar purchases, movies, spa services, or leisure activities are not reimbursable.

## 5. Local Transportation

Reasonable taxi, ride-sharing, public transport, and airport-transfer expenses are reimbursable for approved business travel.

Employees should use company-approved transportation providers where available.

## 6. Meal Expenses

Reasonable meal expenses incurred during approved business travel may be claimed within the applicable daily limits.

Alcoholic beverages are not reimbursable unless explicitly approved as part of an authorized customer or business event.

## 7. Expense Submission

Expense claims must normally be submitted within 30 calendar days of the expense date.

Claims must include appropriate receipts. Employees must provide a clear business purpose for each expense.

Missing receipts require manager approval and may require additional justification.

## 8. Non-Reimbursable Expenses

- Personal entertainment.
- Traffic or parking fines.
- Personal shopping.
- Family travel costs.
- Personal hotel upgrades.
- Personal subscription services.

## 9. Expense Approval

Managers are responsible for confirming that expenses have a legitimate business purpose, comply with company policy, include the required documentation, and are within applicable spending limits.

Finance may reject or return incomplete claims.
